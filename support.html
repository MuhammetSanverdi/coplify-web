Getting started

How do I bring up my clipboard history?

Press ⌘⇧V from any app, or click the Coplify icon in the menu bar. Start typing to filter, then press Return to paste the selected item.

Can I keep something permanently?

Star it. Favorites stay pinned to the top, are never removed automatically, and survive Clear history (keep favorites). Only Clear everything takes them too.

Can I narrow down a long history?

The calendar menu under the search field filters by date, and the menu beside it narrows to one kind of content — text, links, images or files. A toggle switches between newest-first and oldest-first; favorites stay on top either way.

How do I launch Coplify at login?

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → add Coplify.app.

Permissions it may ask for

Accessibility

Lets Coplify press ⌘V for you, so the item you pick lands straight in the app you were using. Decline it and everything still works — the item goes to your clipboard and you paste it yourself.

Screenshot folder

Optional. Point Coplify at the folder macOS saves screenshots to and new captures join your history the moment they're taken — even the ones you never copied. Only that folder is read, and only to look for new screenshots.

Troubleshooting

⌘⇧V doesn't open anything

Another app has probably claimed it — several editors use that combination for paste-without-formatting. Open Coplify's Settings and record a different shortcut; the menu bar icon works regardless.

Items copy, but don't paste automatically

That's the Accessibility permission. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and switch Coplify on. If it's already on, switch it off and back on — macOS occasionally holds on to a stale entry after an app updates.

Screenshots aren't showing up

Coplify can only watch a folder you've handed it yourself. Open Settings and choose the folder macOS saves your screenshots to — that's the Desktop unless you've changed it. If you moved that folder afterwards, choose it again.

Older items disappeared

Coplify removes entries once they pass the retention window in Settings — a week, a month, three months, a year, or forever, and new installs start at one month. Nothing else deletes on its own; set it to forever and nothing is ever removed. Favorites are never trimmed.

Sync isn't picking up my other Mac

Both Macs need to point at the same folder inside a service that's already syncing them — iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or similar. Coplify doesn't move the files itself; it waits for that service to deliver them, so an item appears once the folder has finished syncing. Images stay on the Mac that copied them and are never sent.

Removing Coplify

Drag Coplify out of your Applications folder. Its history, favorites and settings live in its own sandbox container inside ~/Library/Containers/ — delete the Coplify folder there and nothing is left behind. If you'd set up sync, the files it wrote to your shared folder are yours to delete too.
