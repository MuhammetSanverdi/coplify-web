Getting started

How do I bring up my clipboard history?

Press ⌘⇧V from any app, or click the Coplify icon in the menu bar. Start typing to filter, then press Return to put the selected item back on your clipboard.

How do I put something straight into what I'm typing?

Press ⌃⌥V where your cursor already is. Coplify opens there, you pick an item, and the app you are typing in inserts it itself — through macOS's own Services mechanism, so there is nothing to allow and nothing to switch on.

The key belongs to macOS rather than to Coplify, so it is changed in System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services. A few apps do not offer Services at all; in those, drag the row straight out of the panel instead — that route works everywhere, browsers included.

Can I keep something permanently?

Star it. Favorites stay pinned to the top, are never removed automatically, and survive Clear history (keep favorites). Only Clear everything takes them too.

Can I narrow down a long history?

The calendar menu under the search field filters by date, and the menu beside it narrows to one kind of content — text, links, images or files. A toggle switches between newest-first and oldest-first; favorites stay on top either way.

How do I launch Coplify at login?

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → add Coplify.app.

What it asks for

Nothing, to do its job

Coplify requests no permission of any kind. The shortcut, the history, the search and the insertion all work the first time you launch it, and it never appears under System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Screenshot folder

Optional, and only needed if you save screenshots as files. Point Coplify at the folder macOS saves them to and new captures join your history the moment they're taken. Only that folder is read, and only to look for new screenshots.

Press ⌃⌘⇧4 instead and the screenshot goes to your clipboard rather than a file — Coplify keeps it with no folder and no permission at all.

Troubleshooting

⌘⇧V doesn't open anything

Another app has probably claimed it — several editors use that combination for paste-without-formatting. Open Coplify's Settings and record a different shortcut; the menu bar icon works regardless.

⌃⌥V doesn't do anything

That key is a macOS Service, and macOS only offers services from apps installed in /Applications — which is where the Mac App Store puts Coplify. It also appears only when the app you are in has an editable text field focused.

Some apps do not expose Services at all, and a browser window is the common case. There the shortcut simply does nothing; drag the row out of the panel instead, which works everywhere. If the key clashes with something else, change it in System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services.

Screenshots aren't showing up

Two things to check. Screenshot capture is off until you switch it on in Settings — it stays off by default so a fresh install never opens with a permission panel.

If it is on, the folder is probably the wrong one. Coplify can only watch a folder you hand it, and it cannot see where macOS actually saves your screenshots, so the chooser always starts at the Desktop. If you have moved yours, pick the real location — press ⌘⇧5 and open Options to see where that is. Or sidestep it entirely with ⌃⌘⇧4, which copies the screenshot instead of saving it.

Older items disappeared

Coplify removes entries once they pass the retention window in Settings — a week, a month, three months, a year, or forever, and new installs start at one month. Nothing else deletes on its own; set it to forever and nothing is ever removed. Favorites are never trimmed.

Sync isn't picking up my other Mac

Both Macs need to be signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive switched on, and sync turned on in Coplify's settings on each of them — there is nothing else to match up. Coplify doesn't move anything itself; it waits for iCloud, so an item appears once it has come across. Favourites and files stay on the Mac they were copied on. Images travel unless you switch that off.

Removing Coplify

Drag Coplify out of your Applications folder. Its history, favorites and settings live in its own sandbox container inside ~/Library/Containers/ — delete the Coplify folder there and nothing is left behind. Anything sync put in iCloud lives in Coplify's own container and goes with the app — you can also remove it from System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage.
