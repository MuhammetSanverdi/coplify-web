Coplify

Clipboard history that lives in your menu bar.

Coplify keeps a searchable history of everything you copy — text, rich text, images, and files — and gets out of the way otherwise: no Dock icon, no windows left open, just a menu bar item and a shortcut.

Open history from anywhere: ⌘⇧V

Using Coplify

How do I bring up my clipboard history?

Press ⌘⇧V from any app, or click the Coplify icon in the menu bar. Start typing to filter, then press Return to paste the selected item.

Can I keep items permanently?

Click the star next to any item to favorite it. Favorites stay pinned to the top and survive even if you clear your history.

Does it filter by date, or sort differently?

Yes — the calendar menu under the search field filters by Today, Tomorrow, the last 3/7/14/30 days, or All. A sort toggle switches between newest-first and oldest-first; favorites always stay on top.

Where is my history stored?

Locally, in ~/Library/Application Support/Coplify/. Nothing is sent off your Mac unless you explicitly configure a sync folder between your own devices.

How do I launch Coplify at login?

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → add Coplify.app.

Permissions it may ask for

Accessibility

Lets Coplify simulate ⌘V so a selected item pastes automatically. Without it, items still copy to your clipboard — you just press ⌘V yourself.

Screenshot folder access

Lets Coplify notice new screenshots the moment they're saved, so ⌘⇧3/4 captures land in your history automatically — even ones you never copied.
