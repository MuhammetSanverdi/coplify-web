Privacy

Private by design.

Your clipboard contains sensitive data. We treat it that way — everything happens locally on your Mac.

Local processing

Your history stays on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you set up your own sync folder.

No tracking

No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Your usage stays private.

Password-aware

Concealed or transient entries, like one-time passwords, are skipped automatically.

You're in control

Using Coplify

Frequently asked

How do I bring up my clipboard history?

Press ⌘⇧V from any app, or click the Coplify icon in the menu bar. Start typing to filter, then press Return to paste the selected item.

Where is my history stored?

Locally, in ~/Library/Application Support/Coplify/.

How do I launch Coplify at login?

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → add Coplify.app.
