What it does

Built to disappear until you need it

Search as you type

Every copied item is filterable the moment it lands — no browsing a long list.

Date filters

Today, Tomorrow, the last 3/7/14/30 days, or All — from the calendar menu.

Sort your way

Newest or oldest first. Favorites always stay pinned to the top.

Rich content

Plain text, rich text (RTF), images and files — each with a real preview.

Automatic screenshots

⌘⇧3 / ⌘⇧4 captures show up in history the moment they save — even if never copied.

Favorites

Star anything to keep it pinned, even after you clear the rest of your history.

Using Coplify

Frequently asked

How do I bring up my clipboard history?

Press ⌘⇧V from any app, or click the Coplify icon in the menu bar. Start typing to filter, then press Return to paste the selected item.

Where is my history stored?

Locally, in ~/Library/Application Support/Coplify/. Nothing is sent off your Mac unless you explicitly configure a sync folder between your own devices.

How do I launch Coplify at login?

System Settings → General → Login Items → + → add Coplify.app.

Does it capture passwords from my password manager?

No. Clipboard entries marked as concealed or transient by their source app are detected and skipped automatically.
