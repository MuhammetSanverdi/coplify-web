Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

In short: Coplify doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Your clipboard history is stored only on your own Mac, and nothing leaves your device unless you deliberately set up a sync folder between your own computers.

What Coplify stores

Coplify keeps a history of what you copy — text, rich text, images, and file references — so you can search and paste it again later. This history, along with your favorites and preferences, is saved locally at ~/Library/Application Support/Coplify/ on your Mac.

Clipboard entries that the source app marks as concealed or transient (for example, one-time passwords from a password manager) are detected and skipped automatically — they are never written to this history.

What Coplify does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No account, sign-in, or personal identifiers are collected.
  • No data is sent to any server operated by Coplify or anyone else.

Optional screenshot folder access

If you grant it, Coplify watches the folder macOS saves screenshots to, so new screenshots appear in your history automatically. This access is read-only, stays entirely on-device, and is never transmitted anywhere. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Optional device sync

If you choose to enable sync, Coplify writes your clipboard history as files inside a folder you pick yourself — for example, a folder inside iCloud Drive or Dropbox. Coplify does not operate this transport; it only reads and writes files there, using the same account and encryption you already trust for that folder. Turning sync off stops this entirely.

Accessibility permission

Coplify may request the Accessibility permission to simulate ⌘V so a selected item pastes automatically. This permission is used only for that purpose and involves no data collection. Without it, items still copy to your system clipboard — you press ⌘V yourself instead.

Data retention and deletion

History stays on your Mac until you clear it from within Coplify or remove the app. Uninstalling Coplify and deleting ~/Library/Application Support/Coplify/ removes all locally stored data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
